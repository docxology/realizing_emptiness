# GNN integration contract

Use `registry.model_parameters` or `registry.load_model` in every numerical
producer. Preserve the upstream revision in `upstream.json` and the frozen
dependency lock. Register new declarations in `catalog.json` and bind them to a
real consumer with a source-mutation test.

Categorical models declare explicit A/B/C/D tensors and canonical B axes.
Finite native models declare inputs; their mathematical algorithms and
independent acceptance thresholds stay in Python. Practice mapping declarations
remain governance metadata and never drive a simulation.

Run the canonical root checks after changing a declaration, adapter, or consumer.
Regenerate the model audit before figures, manifests, and manuscript outputs.
Keep the [root source contract](../../AGENTS.md) and
[modeling guide](../../docs/gnn-modeling.md) as the shared instructions.
