Scholarship in this manuscript is source-role aware, claim-ID aware, and evidence-ceiling aware. The project separates primary-target claims from background sources and future empirical context, then audits whether each public claim has scoped support plus an explicit prohibited inference and future evidence requirement. A criticality source can justify a proxy vocabulary, for example, but it cannot certify this simulation as a neural measurement; a contemplative-cognition source can motivate an interface vocabulary, but it cannot certify practice efficacy.

The stronger bibliography changes the argument by sharpening boundaries rather than by inflating claims. Discrete active inference and expected-free-energy sources make the `pymdp` loop technically legible. Markov-blanket sources now include the Pearl-blanket/Friston-blanket critique, so boundary language remains a model-use discipline rather than an ontological shortcut [@bruineberg2022emperor]. Relational quantum mechanics and enactive neurophenomenology add background for observer-relative and enacted-interface vocabulary, but they do not make this six-bit screen a physical QRF or an empirical boundary [@rovelli1996relational; @varela1991embodied]. Self-evidencing and computational-phenomenology sources distinguish organismic inferential function, precision reweighting, and identified-with self language without turning the software profile sequence into a claim about realization, therapy, or practice efficacy [@hohwy2026selfevidencingagent; @tal2026advancedmeditation; @prest2026lettinggo; @prest2026selfingwithoutself]. Quantum-trajectory, QRF-transformation, Fine-style local-polytope, Bayesian workflow, simulation-calibration, and reproducibility-checklist sources make the stochastic, frame-covariance, contextuality, BMR, and local artifact-release layers methodologically inspectable [@fine1982hidden; @talts2018sbc; @gelman2020bayesianworkflow; @pineau2020reproducibility]. None of these moves supplies the missing evidence that would be needed for claims about public independent reproduction, human subjects, practice outcomes, neural regimes, or physical qFEP dynamics.

Statistical interpretation begins with an estimand rather than a significance label. Simulation design separates the data-generating mechanism, target quantity, estimator, and performance criterion [@morris2019simulation; @burton2006simulation]. For example, uncertainty in a difference of profile means concerns a contrast; an interval around the mean of pooled profile and control rows concerns a different quantity. Likewise, a dominance effect summarizes ordered comparisons and need not agree with a mean difference. Our methodological implication is to name the contrast and preserve its sampling unit throughout resampling, interval construction, and reporting. Multiple timesteps within a generated trajectory do not become independent replications by being placed in a table. Block-bootstrap theory provides methods for dependent stationary observations, with assumptions and tuning choices that a whole-run bootstrap does not share [@kunsch1989bootstrap; @politis1994stationary]. Citing these methods does not assert that the local audit implements stationary or block bootstrapping.

The null model also determines the scope of a result. A shuffled trajectory conditions on observed values while removing temporal order; rejecting that null establishes sensitivity to order under the chosen construction. It does not identify a critical regime, establish nonlinearity against every linear process, or turn a software trajectory into neural evidence. Surrogate-data methodology and autocorrelation-preserving constructions show why the features preserved by a null must be declared explicitly [@theiler1992surrogate; @schreiber1996surrogate]. When an original trajectory is compared with its own shuffled control, resampling intact pairs preserves that relationship. A paired sign-flip test additionally requires a justified symmetry or randomization argument; pairing alone is insufficient. Permutation methods depend on invariance and exchangeability conditions, including restrictions on permissible rearrangements [@winkler2014permutation; @hemerik2018exact]. Finite random-permutation p-values require the identity-observation convention and a nonzero attainable minimum; a plus-one correction cannot repair an invalid null [@phipson2010permutation]. Multiplicity correction protects a declared family only when its input tests are valid [@romano2005stepdown].

Calibration adds another distinct question. Repeating a seeded generator measures performance under that generator, while the finite number of repetitions introduces Monte Carlo uncertainty [@morris2019simulation; @flegal2008montecarlo]. The latter should not be confused with an interval for a profile contrast or with variability among hypothetical human subjects. Posterior-quantile validation can identify implementation errors, but the sensitivity of a calibration check depends on its test quantity [@cook2006validation; @modrak2025calibration]. Recent posterior simulation-based calibration further distinguishes average prior-predictive behavior from computation conditional on particular data [@sailynoja2026posterior]. These sources motivate adversarial checks, independent seed banks, and explicit calibration targets; they do not establish that a synthetic effect-size positive control is Bayesian posterior calibration. Proper scoring rules offer a complementary criterion for probabilistic predictions, so a narrower distribution or lower entropy alone is not treated as predictive success [@gneiting2007scoring].

The same discipline applies to the active-inference interpretation. Mathematical expositions and minimal agents make assumptions and preference distributions explicit [@buckley2017mathematical; @mcgregor2015minimal; @friston2017process]. Expected free energy is an objective with a particular derivation and decomposition, rather than an automatic consequence of replacing present observations with future ones [@millidge2021whence]. Van Es's instrumentalist argument adds a conceptual counterweight: a model used by a scientist and a representation attributed to an organism are separate commitments [@vanes2021models]. Together with the existing blanket critiques and response, this makes the local generative model an inspectable computational construction without settling the ontology of selfhood.

Quantum-information limits specify a further boundary. Copying a known numerical state description in software is different from implementing a physical channel that clones an arbitrary unknown state or broadcasts a noncommuting family [@wootters1982cloning; @barnum1996broadcast]. Reference-frame resource theories require specified symmetries and allowed operations, with constraints on asymmetry resources and state transformations [@gour2008reference; @marvian2014modes; @marvian2014noether]. Our inference from these sources is that finite relabeling covariance is a useful consistency check, but does not by itself supply a physical reference-frame resource theory or validate the quantum free-energy principle.

Finally, phenomenological vocabulary requires a measurement bridge that this project does not provide. Interview methods explain how descriptions can be elicited and how their validity can be questioned; first-person-data research illustrates a design in which reports constrain a separate analysis rather than replacing it [@petitmengin2006experience; @lutz2002neurophenomenology]. Construct and design critiques caution against identifying questionnaire scores, practice labels, or researcher-defined parameters with a single invariant mindfulness construct [@grossman2008measuring; @davidson2015methodological; @vandam2018hype]. Studies of difficult contemplative experiences also distinguish the description of an experience from its interpretation, valence, duration, and functional impact [@lindahl2017varieties; @britton2021adverse]. For this repository, these are requirements for any future empirical interface, not human-subject results or advice about practice. No simulation profile licenses an inference of safety, compassion, realization, clinical benefit, or neural change.
